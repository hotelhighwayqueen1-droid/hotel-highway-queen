Is there a pure veg restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway near Dudu?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48 at Dudu is a pure vegetarian restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. The whole kitchen is vegetarian, with a separate Jain section made without onion or garlic. It is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, serves Rajasthani thali, paneer dishes, South Indian and pizza, and offers free parking and EV charging.

Pure Veg Restaurant FAQ

Is Hotel Highway Queen restaurant pure veg?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen is a pure vegetarian restaurant on NH-48 at Dudu, and the whole kitchen is vegetarian. Every dish on the menu is vegetarian, so families, pilgrims and vegetarian travellers do not need to check anything before ordering. The menu also has a separate Jain section made without onion and garlic.

Is there a pure veg restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant is on NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur 303008, on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. It is a pure vegetarian restaurant rated 4.8★ on Google from 1000+ reviews. It serves Rajasthani thali, South Indian, pizza, North Indian dishes and chai, with free parking for cars, trucks and buses.

Is Jain food available?

Yes. The menu has a dedicated Jain section made without onion and garlic, which the hotel says is prepared separately. It sits inside a fully vegetarian kitchen. For the full list of Jain dishes and how the section works, see our Jain food page, or call +91 94144 49992 to confirm before you travel.

What is the price of the Queen Special Thali?

The Queen Spl. Thali costs ₹365 plus GST. It comes with dal tadka, mix veg, paneer butter masala, salad, raita, jeera rice, naan, missi roti and a sweet. Menu prices exclude GST and may change, so please confirm current rates with the staff. The Rajasthani thali page has more on the thali itself.

Is it open 24 hours?

Yes. The restaurant is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, with breakfast from 5:30 AM. Hotel guests can also order the full menu to their room around the clock through in-room dining, while the Chinese section is served from 10 AM to 1 AM. Please call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 to confirm before you travel.

Is there parking and EV charging?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is on site and open to any traveller on NH-48, and charging typically takes 20–45 minutes. You can pay with the CHARGE_iN app, Me4U or aggregator apps. Call +91 94144 49992 if you are bringing a large group or several buses.

Is it good for families and senior citizens?

Yes. Families stop here for a full vegetarian menu of thali, dosa, pizza, paneer dishes, mini meals and desserts, with free WiFi and free parking. Because the whole kitchen is vegetarian, there is nothing to check for older guests either. See our pages for families on a road trip and for senior citizens for more detail on staying here.

How do I order or reach the restaurant?

Call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 to order or pre-order. The restaurant is at NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008, on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway, and Google Maps directions are on this page. Cash, UPI and debit or credit cards are accepted. Menu prices exclude GST unless stated and may change, so please confirm with the staff.
