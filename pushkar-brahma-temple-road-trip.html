Frequently Asked Questions

Pushkar Brahma Temple Road Trip:
Your Questions, Answered

How far is Pushkar Brahma Temple from Hotel Highway Queen on the Jaipur road?
+
Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is approximately 65 km from Pushkar Brahma Temple via NH-48. The drive typically takes around 1 hour and 15 minutes by car, passing through Ajmer. This makes us the ideal overnight halt before morning darshan, or a comfortable stopover on your return journey from Pushkar.
Is Brahma Temple Pushkar open every day for darshan?
+
Yes, Brahma Temple Pushkar is open daily for darshan. Morning timings are approximately 6:30 AM to 1:00 PM and evening timings are 3:00 PM to 8:30 PM. Timings may vary during major festivals like Pushkar Mela (Kartik Purnima). Being the world's only prominent Brahma temple, it attracts massive footfall year-round, especially on full moon nights (Purnima).
What is the best time to visit Pushkar and stay on the Jaipur-Ajmer highway?
+
October to March is the best time to visit Pushkar — the weather is pleasant and the Pushkar Lake and ghats are at their most beautiful. The famous Pushkar Camel Fair (Pushkar Mela) takes place in November around Kartik Purnima, making it the most festive and culturally rich time to visit. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu on NH-48 is the ideal base — advance booking is essential during Mela season.
Is vegetarian and Jain food available near the Pushkar route on NH-48?
+
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen | Hotel & Restaurant at Dudu on NH-48 serves 100% vegetarian food including authentic Jain food (no onion, no garlic), Rajasthani thali, South Indian dishes, pizza, and more. The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM and 24hr room service is available. We are widely recognized as the most Jain-pilgrim-friendly hotel on the Jaipur-Ajmer-Pushkar highway.
How do I reach Pushkar from Jaipur via NH-48?
+
From Jaipur, take NH-48 (Jaipur-Ajmer highway) westward. At 45 km, you reach Dudu — where Hotel Highway Queen is located. Continue to Ajmer (60 km from Dudu), then take the Pushkar Road — Pushkar is just 11 km from Ajmer. Total distance Jaipur to Pushkar is approximately 145 km and takes about 2.5 to 3 hours. The road is in excellent condition with multiple petrol stations, eateries, and rest stops along the way.
Is parking available for Pushkar pilgrims at Hotel Highway Queen on the Ajmer road?
+
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu provides free parking for all vehicle types including cars, SUVs, tourist buses, and trucks. The parking area is large, well-lit, and under 24/7 CCTV surveillance. Large pilgrimage groups and temple tour operators frequently use our parking facility. No advance parking fee required — it is complimentary for all guests.
Can I book a room during Pushkar Mela from the Jaipur-Ajmer highway hotel?
+
Yes, but advance booking is strongly recommended during Pushkar Mela (November) as demand is extremely high and all hotels in the region fill up weeks before. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu fills quickly during Mela season. Book via WhatsApp at +91-94144-49992 or through Booking.com, Agoda, or MakeMyTrip.
