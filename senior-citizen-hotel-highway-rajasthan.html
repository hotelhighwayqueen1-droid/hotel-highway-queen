Answers to the most common questions from elderly travelers, their families, and senior pilgrims planning a stop at Hotel Highway Queen on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway.

Are there ground floor rooms available on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway at Hotel Highway Queen? ▾
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48 at Dudu offers ground floor room options that are ideal for senior citizens and guests with limited mobility. You do not have to climb multiple flights of stairs to reach your room. Parking is directly accessible close to the main entrance, and staff are available to assist with luggage. Please call us on +91-94144-49992 before arrival to request a ground floor room, as these are allocated on a first-come basis and pre-requesting ensures we have one ready for you.
Is lift or elevator access available at Hotel Highway Queen for elderly guests? ▾
Hotel Highway Queen is a highway property designed for ease of access. Ground floor rooms are available and strongly recommended for senior guests and those with mobility concerns — these rooms are directly accessible without using stairs or a lift. Please contact us before your arrival on +91-94144-49992 so we can assign the most accessible room for your needs. Our staff are available 24 hours to assist elderly guests with any mobility requirements.
Is parking close to the rooms for senior and elderly guests on Ajmer road? ▾
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen offers free parking on premises. The parking lot is located directly adjacent to the property entrance and is spacious enough for cars, SUVs, trucks, and buses. For senior guests, this means minimal walking from vehicle to room — a key convenience that many other NH-48 stops do not offer. The parking area is well-lit and covered by CCTV security, so your vehicle is safe overnight.
Is Jain food available at Hotel Highway Queen for senior vegetarian travelers? ▾
Yes. Jain food is available at Hotel Highway Queen's restaurant, which opens at 5:30 AM. Jain meals are prepared without root vegetables — no potato, onion, garlic, or underground vegetables — in adherence to Jain dietary principles. In-room Jain food service is also available 24 hours a day via our room service. For pilgrims traveling to Ranakpur Jain temple, Ajmer, or Pushkar, this is one of the most thoughtful food options on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. We recommend informing us of this requirement when you call to book.
Can senior citizens get 24-hour room service at this hotel on NH-48? ▾
Yes. This is one of the most valued features for senior travelers at Hotel Highway Queen. Our 24-hour in-room dining service ensures elderly guests never have to leave the comfort and safety of their room at odd hours to find food. Whether it is 3 AM chai after a late-night arrival from Delhi, an early morning dal-roti before 4 AM departure for Ajmer namaz, or a light diet meal for a diabetic senior — just call reception and we deliver to your room. This 24-hour service is available all days, including during festivals and pilgrimage seasons.
Is Hotel Highway Queen accessible for guests with limited mobility or physical difficulty? ▾
Hotel Highway Queen is designed as a highway hospitality property with ease of access in mind. Ground floor rooms reduce or eliminate stair-climbing. The parking lot connects directly to the entrance. The restaurant is conveniently located. Our staff operate 24 hours and are experienced in assisting senior guests and those with physical difficulty. We strongly recommend calling us in advance on +91-94144-49992 to discuss your specific needs — we are happy to make arrangements including luggage assistance, ground floor allocation, and dedicated room service scheduling.
Is there a doctor on call near Dudu, Rajasthan for medical emergencies? ▾
The hotel staff can assist in contacting local medical services in Dudu town in case of a medical need. Dudu has pharmacies and general practitioners nearby. For senior travelers with specific medical conditions, we recommend carrying your prescriptions and medications with you. In case of a serious medical emergency, Jaipur — with major hospitals including SMS Hospital and Fortis Jaipur — is approximately 45 km away on NH-48 and can typically be reached in under 60 minutes. Our front desk operates 24 hours and will assist immediately if any guest requires emergency support.
Is light or diet-friendly food available for senior citizens at this hotel on Jaipur road? ▾
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen's restaurant offers a wide range of options suitable for senior dietary needs. Our menu includes: light South Indian options such as idli and upma, simple dal-roti, fresh chai and biscuits, Rajasthani dishes with lighter preparations on request, Jain sattvic meals, and plain rice-dal for those on medical diets. In-room dining runs 24 hours, so a senior guest who wakes at 5 AM and needs something light before medication can simply call reception. For current menu options and seasonal specials, please contact us directly — we are happy to discuss and accommodate your dietary requirements.
