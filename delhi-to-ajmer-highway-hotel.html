Why Stop at Dudu — The Perfect Midpoint

Dudu is roughly 290 km from Delhi and just 60 km short of Ajmer — meaning you finish the tiring Delhi–Jaipur stretch, rest fully, and arrive in Ajmer fresh the next morning instead of driving 350+ km in one exhausting stretch. Hotel Highway Queen sits directly on NH-48 in Ram Nagar, Dudu, so there's no detour off the highway — pull in, park, rest, and get back on the road.

Delhi to Ajmer Highway Hotel — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best hotel to stop between Delhi and Ajmer?

Hotel Highway Queen in Dudu is the best-placed stop, sitting almost exactly halfway between Delhi and Ajmer, directly on NH-48, roughly 290 km from Delhi and 60 km short of Ajmer.

How far is Dudu from Delhi and from Ajmer?

Dudu is approximately 290 km from Delhi and approximately 60 km from Ajmer, making it the natural midpoint stop on the Delhi–Jaipur–Ajmer highway route.

Is Hotel Highway Queen directly on the NH-48 highway?

Yes. The hotel is located on NH-48 itself, in Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur — so there's no need to exit onto local roads to reach it.

Does Hotel Highway Queen have parking for trucks and buses?

Yes, the hotel offers free parking and welcomes trucks and buses in addition to cars, making it suitable for commercial drivers and group travel.

Is food available late at night on the Delhi to Ajmer route?

Yes. The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM and in-room dining is available 24 hours a day, so food is available whenever you arrive.

Does the hotel have EV charging on the Delhi–Ajmer highway?

Yes, Hotel Highway Queen has a Mahindra CHARGE_iN fast charger with a CCS2 connector on site for electric vehicles.

What room types and prices are available?

The hotel offers Deluxe rooms at ₹2,200, Balcony rooms at ₹2,400, and Super Deluxe rooms at ₹2,700 per night, all air-conditioned.

Should I stop overnight between Delhi and Ajmer, or drive straight through?

Driving straight from Delhi to Ajmer covers roughly 390 km, which is tiring in a single stretch. Splitting the journey with a stop in Dudu, close to the Jaipur side, lets you rest and continue to Ajmer fresh the next day.

How do I book a room at Hotel Highway Queen?

You can book directly via WhatsApp or phone at +91-94144-49992, or through Agoda, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Expedia, Goibibo or JustDial.

Is Hotel Highway Queen a good stop for Pushkar or Ajmer Dargah visitors?

Yes. At around 60 km from Ajmer, the hotel makes a convenient overnight base for pilgrims and travellers heading to the Ajmer Dargah or Pushkar the next morning.
