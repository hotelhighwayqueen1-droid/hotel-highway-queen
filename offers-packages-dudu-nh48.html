Restaurant at Hotel Highway Queen

Pure-veg restaurant at Hotel Highway Queen, Dudu: Rajasthani thali, dedicated Jain food, South Indian, pizza, North Indian and chai, with free parking for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses.

The restaurant is part of Hotel Highway Queen, directly on NH-48 in Ram Nagar, Dudu. Stop for a thali, a dosa, a pizza or a cup of chai, and stay in one of the 26 AC rooms if you want to rest before the next stretch. Quick questions

Are the room rates inclusive of GST?

No. The rates shown (₹2,200, ₹2,400 and ₹2,700 per night) are the original rates, and GST is extra.

What does the 30% OFF badge mean?

It shows that an offer is running on that room type. The hotel confirms the offer dates, who can use it and room availability when you call or WhatsApp, so please check before you book.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Reception is open 24 hours with hotel staff available. Early check-in depends on availability.

Is the restaurant open 24 hours?

Yes, the restaurant is open 24 hours.

Can I enquire for a wedding or a group?

Yes. Send your dates and number of guests on WhatsApp or call reception, and the hotel will confirm what is available.

Restaurant questions

Which is the best restaurant in Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant on NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur 303008 is rated 4.8★ from 1,000+ Google reviews. It serves Rajasthani thali, dedicated Jain food, South Indian, pizza, North Indian and chai, with free parking for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses.

Is late-night food available near Dudu on NH-48?

Hotel guests can order the full menu to their room 24/7 through in-room dining. The Chinese section is served from 10 AM to 1 AM, and the restaurant is open 24 hours.

Is Jain food available in Dudu?

Yes. The menu has a dedicated Jain section made without onion and garlic, which the hotel says is prepared separately. Examples are Jain Dal Tadka ₹260, Jain Palak Paneer ₹320, Jain Shahi Paneer in white gravy ₹350 and Jain Corn Palak ₹290 (GST extra).

How much is the Rajasthani thali in Dudu?

The Queen Spl. Thali costs ₹365 plus GST. It comes with dal tadka, mix veg, paneer butter masala, salad, raita, jeera rice, naan, missi roti and a sweet.

Is there parking for cars, trucks and buses?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses. Call +91-94144-49992 if you are bringing a large group or several buses.

Is the restaurant family friendly?

Yes. Families stop here for a full vegetarian menu of thali, dosa, pizza, paneer dishes, mini meals and desserts. The property also has free WiFi and free parking, and birthday or family get-together events can be arranged on WhatsApp.

Is EV charging available at the restaurant?

Yes. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is on site and open to any traveller on NH-48. Charging typically takes 20–45 minutes, and you can pay with the CHARGE_iN app, Me4U or aggregator apps.

Can I book a room at Hotel Highway Queen?

Yes. There are 26 AC rooms: Deluxe ₹2,200, Deluxe Balcony ₹2,400 and Super Deluxe ₹2,700 per night plus GST. Book on WhatsApp at +91-94144-49992 or on Agoda, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Expedia or Goibibo; cancellation is free up to 24 hours before check-in.

How can I pay at the restaurant?

Cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards are accepted. Menu prices exclude GST unless stated and may change, so please confirm current rates with the staff.
