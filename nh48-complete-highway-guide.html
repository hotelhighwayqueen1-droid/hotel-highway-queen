Frequently Asked Questions

NH-48 & Hotel FAQ

What is the total length and route of NH-48? +
National Highway 48 (NH-48) spans approximately 2,807 km from Delhi to Chennai, passing through Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu. It was previously designated NH-8 (Delhi–Mumbai) and NH-4 (Mumbai–Chennai) before renumbering in 2010. The full route: Delhi → Gurugram → Kotputli → Jaipur → Dudu → Kishangarh → Ajmer → Beawar → Chittorgarh → Udaipur → Ahmedabad → Vadodara → Surat → Mumbai → Pune → Belagavi → Bengaluru → Vellore → Chennai.
Which cities does NH-48 pass through in Rajasthan? +
In Rajasthan, NH-48 passes through: Kotputli → Jaipur (State Capital) → Dudu → Kishangarh → Nasirabad → Ajmer (Dargah Sharif) → Beawar → Gulabpura → Chittorgarh (UNESCO fort) → Udaipur (City of Lakes). Hotel Highway Queen in Dudu is the top-rated hotel stop on this Rajasthan stretch — 45 km from Jaipur, 60 km from Ajmer.
What is the best hotel on NH-48 between Jaipur and Ajmer? +
Hotel Highway Queen in Dudu, Rajasthan is the highest-rated hotel on NH-48 between Jaipur and Ajmer — 4.8★ across 1,000+ Google reviews. How far is Delhi from Jaipur on NH-48? +
Delhi to Jaipur via NH-48 is approximately 270–280 km, typically a 3.5 to 4-hour drive. The route goes through Gurugram, Manesar, Kotputli, and Shahpura before reaching Jaipur. Total toll charges for a car (FASTag) are approximately ₹400. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is 45 km further, making the Delhi → Dudu distance approximately 315 km (about 4 hours).
How far is Ahmedabad from Jaipur on NH-48? +
Ahmedabad to Jaipur via NH-48 is approximately 670 km — a 9 to 10-hour drive passing through Udaipur, Chittorgarh, Beawar, Ajmer, and Kishangarh. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is 45 km before Jaipur, making it an ideal overnight stop at the 625 km mark from Ahmedabad. It's the last well-rated hotel before Jaipur with guaranteed 24-hour check-in.
What toll plazas are on NH-48 between Delhi and Jaipur? +
The main toll plazas on NH-48 between Delhi and Jaipur include: Kherki Daula (near Gurugram, ~₹95), Shahjahanpur Toll (~₹155), and Bagru Toll (~₹150). Total for a car (FASTag) is approximately ₹400 one-way. FASTag is mandatory on all NHAI roads — vehicles without FASTag pay double. A GPS-based toll system (GNSS) is being introduced in phases from 2025. For real-time toll rates, use TollGuru or iHMCL's official website.
Which route is best from Jodhpur or Jaisalmer to Jaipur via NH-48? +
From Jodhpur (~330 km): Drive via Pali → Beawar → Ajmer, then join NH-48 through Kishangarh toward Jaipur. From Jaisalmer (~570 km): Drive via Jodhpur → same route. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is 45 km before Jaipur on NH-48 — the natural last stop before the Jaipur city approaches, ideal for a meal, fuel, or overnight stay before entering the city.

Is NH-48 the same as old NH-8? +
Yes, partially. The Delhi to Mumbai stretch of NH-48 was previously designated NH-8 under the old numbering system. The Mumbai to Chennai stretch was designated NH-4. When NHAI renumbered all national highways in 2010–2012, both were merged and renumbered as NH-48 — connecting Delhi to Chennai as a single continuous highway of 2,807 km. The physical road and alignment remained the same; only the designation changed.
