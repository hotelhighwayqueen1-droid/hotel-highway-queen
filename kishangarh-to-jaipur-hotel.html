About Kishangarh

Why Travelers Visit Kishangarh

✈️ Kishangarh Airport (KQH)

Rajasthan's 3rd airport, serving Delhi and other domestic routes. Hotel Highway Queen is the closest well-rated NH-48 hotel to KQH — 30 km, ~35 min. 5:30AM restaurant covers early morning departures. 24hr check-in for late-night arrivals.

Kishangarh — Marble Capital of India

  • 💎
    Marble & Granite Market: Kishangarh is Asia's largest marble and granite trading hub. Thousands of traders, suppliers, and buyers visit annually — all traveling NH-48.
  • 🏰
    Kishangarh Fort: The historic 17th-century fort overlooking Gundalao Lake, best photographed at sunrise. Hotel Highway Queen's 5:30AM restaurant makes a dawn visit easy.
  • 🎨
    Kishangarh Miniature Paintings: One of India's most celebrated classical painting traditions. Famous for the distinctive Bani Thani portrait — now a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage.
  • 🏫
    Educational Hub: Kishangarh hosts multiple colleges and the nearby Central University of Rajasthan (38 km from our hotel) — a steady stream of student and faculty travelers on NH-48.
  • 🛣
    Kishangarh Bypass: The NH-48 Kishangarh bypass connects directly to Ajmer without entering the city — a key reason so many travelers use the Kishangarh–Dudu–Jaipur corridor.
