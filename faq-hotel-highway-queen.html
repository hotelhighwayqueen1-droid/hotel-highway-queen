🕐 Check-In, Check-Out & Timings
What is the check-in time at Hotel Highway Queen?
Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM (noon). However, since the hotel operates a genuinely staffed 24-hour front desk, early check-in is accommodated based on room availability — no fee if the room is free. Guests arriving at 2 AM, 4 AM, or any other hour can check in without any advance notice. Just call +91-94144-49992 to confirm.
What is the check-out time at Hotel Highway Queen?
Standard check-out time is 10:00 AM. Late check-out beyond 10 AM is available on request, subject to room availability, and may be charged extra. Contact the front desk the evening before to arrange this.
Does Hotel Highway Queen have 24-hour check-in? Can I arrive late at night?
Yes, absolutely. Hotel Highway Queen operates a staffed 24-hour front desk every day of the year. There is no curfew, no locked gate at night, and no penalty for arriving late. Highway travelers who decide to stop at 1 AM, 3 AM, or 5 AM can pull in, call the desk, and check in within minutes. No advance booking is required for same-night walk-in arrivals — though calling ahead to confirm availability is always a good idea: +91-94144-49992.
Is early check-in possible? Is there an extra charge?
Early check-in before 12:00 PM is possible subject to room availability. If your room has been cleaned and vacated by the previous guest, early check-in is offered at no extra charge. If a room needs to be assigned before the standard turnover, the hotel may request a nominal early-check-in fee. Call ahead at +91-94144-49992 to confirm.
Do I need a valid ID to check in at Hotel Highway Queen?
Yes. A valid government-issued photo ID is mandatory at check-in as per government regulations. Accepted IDs include: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, Voter ID, or PAN Card. This applies to all guests including walk-in arrivals.

🍽 Restaurant & Dining
What time does the restaurant open at Hotel Highway Queen?
The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM every day — one of the earliest kitchen opening times on the entire NH-48 Jaipur–Ajmer corridor. Whether you need to leave early for Ajmer Dargah's morning prayers, continue toward Jaipur before traffic builds, or simply prefer an early breakfast, the kitchen is ready. In-room dining is available 24 hours a day. See the Restaurant page for the full menu.
Is non-vegetarian food available at Hotel Highway Queen?
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen serves both vegetarian and non-vegetarian food daily. The menu includes Rajasthani thali, North Indian curries, Mughlai dishes, tandoori items, dal baati churma, gatte ki sabzi, highway snacks, chai, cold beverages, and packaged food. The hotel's motto — "बिन चखे न जाना, क्वीन का खाना" — reflects the restaurant's pride in its food.
Can I order food to my room at night?
Yes — in-room dining is available 24 hours a day. Whether you arrive at midnight or 4 AM, you can order from the full menu including hot meals, snacks, chai, and beverages. Call the front desk from your room to place an order at any hour.
Is breakfast included in the room rate?
Standard room rates at Hotel Highway Queen are room-only (without breakfast included). Breakfast is available à la carte from 5:30 AM in the restaurant. The menu includes continental breakfast options (fresh bread, fruit, juice), North Indian options, and chai. For special breakfast-included packages, contact the hotel directly.

⚡ EV Charging — Mahindra CHARGE_iN
Does Hotel Highway Queen have EV charging?
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen has a Mahindra CHARGE_iN DC fast charging station on the property, operational 24/7. It uses a CCS2 connector and is compatible with all major EVs on Indian roads. Hotel Highway Queen is one of the very few properties on the NH-48 Jaipur–Ajmer corridor with dedicated EV fast charging infrastructure.
Which EV cars can charge at Hotel Highway Queen?
The CCS2 connector at Hotel Highway Queen is compatible with: Mahindra BE 6, XEV 9e, XUV400 EV, XUV 3XO EV · Tata Nexon EV, Tata Punch EV, Tata Curvv EV · MG ZS EV, MG Windsor EV · Hyundai Creta EV · Kia EV6 · and all other CCS2-compatible electric vehicles. Call +91-94144-49992 to confirm availability before a long journey.
How long does EV charging take at Hotel Highway Queen?
The Mahindra CHARGE_iN is a DC fast charger. For Mahindra EVs (BE 6, XEV 9e, XUV400), expect approximately 20–30 minutes from 20% to 80% SoC. Other CCS2 vehicles will vary depending on battery capacity and onboard charger rating. For a full overnight charge, simply check into one of the 26 AC rooms and wake up to a full battery — then start fresh with breakfast at 5:30 AM.
How do I pay for EV charging at Hotel Highway Queen?
Payment for EV charging at Hotel Highway Queen is made via the Mahindra CHARGE_iN app, the Me4U aggregator app, or other partner payment platforms. No cash payment at the charger — use the app on your smartphone. The charger is available 24/7 without any booking requirement.
Is Hotel Highway Queen good for Delhi to Jaipur or Delhi to Ajmer EV road trips?
Yes — it is the ideal EV charging stop on this corridor. Delhi to Jaipur is ~270 km. Hotel Highway Queen is 45 km beyond Jaipur — approximately 315 km from Delhi. For the Jaipur-to-Ajmer extension, the hotel's CHARGE_iN station is the best-equipped fast charging stop between the two cities. For EV road trippers on the full Delhi → Jaipur → Ajmer → Pushkar → Udaipur corridor, Hotel Highway Queen is the critical charge point before Kishangarh and Ajmer.
