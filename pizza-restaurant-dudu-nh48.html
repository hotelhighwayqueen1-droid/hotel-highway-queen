Is pizza available on the Jaipur-Ajmer highway?
Yes — Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu, NH-48 is one of the very few places on the Jaipur-Ajmer highway that serves fresh pizza. This isn't packaged or frozen pizza — it's made fresh in our kitchen. The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM, and pizza is available throughout the day. Staying guests can also order pizza through our 24-hour in-room dining service at any hour.
Where can I get pizza between Jaipur and Ajmer?
Hotel Highway Queen | Hotel & Restaurant, located at NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Rajasthan 303008 is the best-known spot for pizza on this route. We're positioned at 45 km from Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer — perfect as a midway stop. Look for us on the left side of NH-48 coming from Jaipur, with clearly visible signage and large parking space. Google Maps coordinates: 26.6611729, 75.1876777.
Is pizza available at night on NH-48?
For staying guests, yes — 24-hour in-room dining is available, including pizza. Our restaurant itself opens at 5:30 AM. Whether you're a night trucker, a late-arriving family, or an early-morning traveler, our kitchen team is ready to serve. Call ahead at +91-94144-49992 to confirm availability for late-night orders.
What kind of pizza is served at Hotel Highway Queen?
We serve vegetarian pizza made fresh to order. Our kitchen uses fresh dough, sauce, and toppings — no pre-made frozen bases. The pizza pairs brilliantly with our other menu items if you want a mixed spread. For specific topping options, seasonal specials, or current menu pricing, please call us or WhatsApp us — we're happy to share the full menu.
Is there a kids' food menu on the Ajmer road?
Hotel Highway Queen is extremely family-friendly. Our multi-cuisine menu is naturally suited for kids — pizza is always a winner, and we also serve South Indian dosas, plain parathas, rice dishes, and chai with biscuits. We have large, clean dining space where families can sit comfortably. Families traveling from Jaipur to Ajmer, Pushkar, or beyond often specifically stop here because the menu works for both adults and children.
Can I order pizza in the room at Hotel Highway Queen?
Yes, absolutely. All 26 AC rooms at Hotel Highway Queen have 24-hour in-room dining available. Just call the front desk or the restaurant extension from your room and order anything from the menu — pizza, thali, South Indian, chai, whatever you like. It'll be brought straight to your room. This is one of the features our guests love most — waking up in the middle of the night and being able to order real food.
Is it veg pizza on the Jaipur-Ajmer road?
Yes — our pizza is vegetarian. Hotel Highway Queen is also one of the few highway restaurants that serves certified Jain food (no onion, no garlic). This makes us the go-to stop for Jain families and travelers who need pure vegetarian food on the Jaipur-Ajmer-Pushkar route. We take food purity seriously, which is reflected in our 4.8★ rating.
