FAQs

Jaipur to Pushkar Hotel Stop — Questions

How far is Hotel Highway Queen from Pushkar?+

Hotel Highway Queen sits on NH-48 in Dudu, roughly 75–80 km from Pushkar via the Ajmer bypass road — about a 1 hr 45 min to 2 hour drive. It's also 45 km from Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer, making it a natural halfway halt on the Jaipur–Pushkar route.

What is the best route from Jaipur to Pushkar by road?+

The common route is Jaipur → Dudu on NH-48 (45 km) → Ajmer on the same highway (~60 km) → Pushkar via the Ajmer–Pushkar road over the Snake Mountain pass (~15 km). Total distance is approximately 145 km, taking about 2.5–3 hours.

Is it worth breaking the Jaipur to Pushkar journey with an overnight stop?+

Yes, especially during Pushkar Mela season or when travelling with family or at night. Dudu sits almost exactly at the midpoint of the Jaipur–Ajmer stretch, so halting here lets you rest, eat properly, and reach Pushkar fresh rather than driving straight through.

When is the Pushkar Camel Fair (Pushkar Mela) 2026?+

Pushkar Mela 2026 is scheduled for approximately 17–24 November 2026, climaxing on Kartik Purnima. Hotel prices in Pushkar spike sharply during this window, so many travellers base themselves at Hotel Highway Queen in Dudu instead.

Is there a hotel with EV charging on the way to Pushkar from Jaipur?+

Yes — Hotel Highway Queen has a Mahindra CHARGE_iN DC fast charger with a CCS2 connector, compatible with most EVs including Mahindra, Tata and MG models, so you can top up while resting or eating.

Does the hotel have parking for tour buses and large groups heading to Pushkar Mela?+

Yes, free and secure parking is available 24/7 for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses — a practical stop for tour groups and pilgrims heading to Pushkar.

What are the room rates near the Jaipur-Pushkar route?+

Deluxe rooms start at ₹2,200/night, Deluxe Balcony rooms are ₹2,400/night, and Super Deluxe rooms are ₹2,700/night, plus taxes — all with free WiFi, Smart LED TV, AC and 24-hour room service.

Is Hotel Highway Queen open for late-night check-in coming from Pushkar or Ajmer?+

Yes, reception operates 24/7 with no restrictions on check-in time, and the restaurant offers 24-hour in-room dining for travellers arriving late from Pushkar Mela or a night drive.

How far is the hotel from Ajmer Junction, the nearest station to Pushkar?+

Hotel Highway Queen is about 60 km from Ajmer Junction — the nearest major railway station to Pushkar. Guests arriving by train to Ajmer often halt here before continuing to Pushkar by road.
