Restaurant & Hotel on NH-48: Your Questions

Quick answers for travellers on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway.

Which is the best restaurant on NH-48 near Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant at Ram Nagar, Dudu is the highest-rated stop on this stretch of NH-48, with a 4.8★ Google rating and 1,000+ reviews. It serves Rajasthani thali, South Indian, pizza, North Indian and Jain food from a pure vegetarian kitchen.

Is there a 24-hour restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with breakfast service from 5:30 AM. Call +91 94144 49992 at any hour to confirm.

Is there a hotel with a restaurant in Dudu?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen at Ram Nagar, Dudu has 26 air-conditioned rooms (Deluxe, Balcony and Super Deluxe), reception staffed 24 hours and a 24-hour restaurant on the same property, so you can check in and eat without leaving the highway.

Which hotel on the Jaipur–Ajmer route has free parking and EV charging?

Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48 at Dudu offers free parking for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses and a Mahindra CHARGE_iN EV charger on the property. It is rated 4.8★ on Google by 1,000+ guests.

Is the food pure vegetarian, and is Jain food available?

Yes. The kitchen is pure vegetarian, and the Queen Menu has a dedicated Jain section with dal tadka, palak paneer, shahi paneer, jeera aloo and corn palak, all without onion or garlic. Message us on WhatsApp for group orders.

Can I get South Indian food and pizza on the highway?

Yes. Masala dosa, Mysore masala dosa, idli and uttapam are served 24 hours, and the pizza corner offers Margherita, tandoori paneer and garden fresh pizzas.

How much does a meal cost at Hotel Highway Queen?

The Queen Special Thali is ₹365, Masala Dosa ₹240, Margherita Pizza ₹270 and Masala Tea ₹35. Prices exclude GST and may change, so check the Queen Menu.

Is there parking for trucks and buses?

Yes. Free parking is available for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses, and the Mahindra CHARGE_iN EV charger is on the property, so you can charge while you eat.

Can I pre-book meals for a bus or a group?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to pre-book thali, Jain meals or a chai stop for your group. Free bus parking is available.

How do I find a restaurant or hotel near me on NH-48 at Dudu?

Search Hotel Highway Queen on Google Maps or tap Get Directions below. We are at Ram Nagar, Dudu on NH-48, the Jaipur–Ajmer highway, and the restaurant is open 24 hours, so you can stop at any hour. Call +91 94144 49992 if you need help finding the entrance.

How do I reach the restaurant?

It is on NH-48 at Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008, on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. Tap Get Directions below or call +91 94144 49992.
