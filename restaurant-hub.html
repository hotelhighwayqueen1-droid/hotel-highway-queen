Restaurant on NH-48: Your Questions

Quick answers for travellers on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway.

Which is the best restaurant on NH-48 near Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant at Ram Nagar, Dudu is the highest-rated stop on this stretch of NH-48, with a 4.8★ Google rating and 1,000+ reviews. It serves Rajasthani thali, South Indian, pizza, North Indian and Jain food.

Is there a 24-hour restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with breakfast service from 5:30 AM. Call +91 94144 49992 at any hour to confirm.

Is Jain food available on NH-48?

Yes. The Queen Menu has a dedicated Jain section with dal tadka, palak paneer, shahi paneer, jeera aloo and corn palak, all without onion or garlic. Message us on WhatsApp for group orders.

Can I get South Indian food and pizza on the highway?

Yes. Masala dosa, Mysore masala dosa, idli and uttapam are served 24 hours, and the pizza corner offers Margherita, tandoori paneer and garden fresh pizzas.

How much does a meal cost at Hotel Highway Queen?

The Queen Special Thali is ₹365, Masala Dosa ₹240, Margherita Pizza ₹270 and Masala Tea ₹35. Prices exclude GST and may change, so check the Queen Menu.

Is there parking for trucks and EV charging?

Yes. Free parking is available for cars, trucks and buses, and a Mahindra CHARGE_iN EV charger is on the property.

How do I reach the restaurant?

It is on NH-48 at Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008, roughly 65 km from Jaipur and 65 km from Ajmer. Tap Get Directions below or call +91 94144 49992.
