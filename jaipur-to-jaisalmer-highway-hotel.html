Road Trip Guide

Jaipur → Ajmer → Jodhpur → Jaisalmer
The Complete Route Breakdown

Every stop on the desert highway — distances, what to expect, and how Hotel Highway Queen fits into your journey. Whether you're making this in one day or spreading across two, here's your roadmap.

01

Jaipur — The Pink City (Start)

0 km · Start Point Recommended Departure: 5:00–7:00 AM

Exit Jaipur early to avoid city traffic. Head southwest on NH-48 (Ajmer Road / Jaipur–Kishangarh Expressway). The expressway is 6-lane and smooth. Pack fuel — the next reliable fuel point is at Dudu or Kishangarh.

02

Dudu — Hotel Highway Queen

45 km · ~45 min from Jaipur NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu

The first premium rest stop on your Jaipur to Jaisalmer journey. Perfect for an early breakfast (restaurant opens 5:30 AM), EV charging, fuel refueling, a fresh start if you drove overnight, or a full overnight stay. Free parking for all vehicles. Staff at the front desk always.

👑 YOU STOP HERE
03

Kishangarh — The Marble City

75 km from Jaipur · 30 km from Dudu ~25 min from Hotel Highway Queen

Famous for Kishangarh miniature paintings ("Bani Thani"), the marble and granite trade, and the scenic Gundolav Lake. The Kishangarh bypass connects you to Ajmer without entering the city. Quick fuel stop options available.

04

Ajmer & Pushkar — Spiritual Heart of Rajasthan

~105 km from Jaipur · 60 km from Dudu ~1 hr from Hotel Highway Queen

Ajmer Sharif Dargah — the shrine of Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti — draws millions of pilgrims annually. Ana Sagar Lake and Taragarh Fort are worth an hour each. Pushkar (15 km further) has the sacred Pushkar Lake, 52 ghats, and the famous Brahma Temple. Plan 2–4 hours here before continuing.

05

Beawar — NH-48 Junction

~165 km from Jaipur Highway continues westward

Beawar is the junction where NH-48 splits toward Jodhpur (NH-25/62) and toward Udaipur (NH-58). Most Jaisalmer-bound travelers continue on NH-62 via Pali toward Jodhpur. Fuel up in Beawar — quality stations available.

06

Jodhpur — The Blue City (Overnight Recommended)

~335 km from Jaipur · 230 km from Dudu ~4 hrs from Hotel Highway Queen

Mehrangarh Fort dominates the Jodhpur skyline — one of India's most magnificent forts. The blue-painted old city below is a photographer's paradise. Umaid Bhawan Palace Museum is also here. We strongly recommend an overnight stop in Jodhpur — it deserves at least a half-day. Continue to Jaisalmer fresh the next morning.

07

Pokhran & Osian

~470 km from Jaipur Desert territory begins

Pokhran is famous in history (India's nuclear test site). Osian, 65 km before Jaisalmer, has ancient temples dating back centuries. The landscape has fully shifted to Thar Desert by now — sandy stretches, sparse villages, camel sightings. Fuel stations become sparse — fill up in Jodhpur.

08

Jaisalmer — The Golden City

~600 km from Jaipur · ~555 km from Dudu Total Drive: 9–11 hours

The living Jaisalmer Fort, Sam Sand Dunes, camel safaris at sunset, rooftop dinners overlooking the golden city — Jaisalmer is unlike anywhere else in India. Plan 2–3 nights minimum. The journey from Hotel Highway Queen to Jaisalmer is better done in 2 days with an overnight in Jodhpur.

What is the best hotel on the Jaipur to Jaisalmer highway?

Where is the first stop on the Jaipur to Jaisalmer road trip?

+
Dudu on NH-48 (45 km from Jaipur) is the first major rest stop. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is the ideal overnight or meal stop before continuing to Ajmer (60 km ahead), Jodhpur (230 km), and Jaisalmer (~555 km).

Does Hotel Highway Queen have EV charging?

+
Yes — Hotel Highway Queen has a Mahindra CHARGE_iN fast charger (CCS2) on property, installed in 2026. It's the first EV charging point on this stretch of NH-48 between Jaipur and Kishangarh. Compatible with Tata Nexon EV, Mahindra XEV, MG ZS EV, and other CCS2 vehicles.

What time does the restaurant open?

The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM every day — ideal for early starters heading to Ajmer for morning prayers, or pilgrims and highway drivers who set off before dawn. In-room dining is available 24 hours. Menu includes Rajasthani thali, dal baati churma, North Indian, and snacks.

Is parking free for trucks and buses?

Yes. Parking is 100% free for all vehicle types — cars, SUVs, trucks, buses, and commercial vehicles. The parking area is spacious with direct NH-48 access. No booking required for parking.

Is there 24-hour check-in at Hotel Highway Queen?

Yes. The front desk is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Arrive at 2 AM after a long drive from Delhi or Ahmedabad — the hotel is always ready. No surcharge for late night check-in.

How far is Hotel Highway Queen from Ajmer Sharif Dargah?

Hotel Highway Queen is approximately 60 km from Ajmer city and the Ajmer Sharif Dargah. From Dudu on NH-48, continue on the expressway toward Kishangarh (30 km), then 30 km further into Ajmer. Total drive time is approximately 50–60 minutes.
