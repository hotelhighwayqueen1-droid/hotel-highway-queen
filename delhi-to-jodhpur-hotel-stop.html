Under normal traffic conditions, Dudu is approximately 4.5 to 5.5 hours from Delhi via NH-48 (Delhi–Jaipur Expressway). We recommend departing Delhi between 5 AM – 7 AM to avoid city traffic and arrive at Dudu by late morning or early afternoon. Weekend traffic on the expressway can add 30–60 minutes.
