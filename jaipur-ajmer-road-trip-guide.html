Places to Visit

Best Stops on the
Jaipur–Ajmer Highway

The NH-48 corridor is one of Rajasthan's richest road trip routes — sacred shrines, fortress ruins, marble towns, and sacred lakes within 120 km.

0 km from Jaipur

🏰 Jaipur — The Pink City

Start your trip from Rajasthan's capital. Visit Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar before hitting the highway. Depart early morning (6–8 AM) to avoid city traffic and reach Dudu in time for breakfast.

Amber Fort Hawa Mahal City Palace

20 km from Jaipur

🎨 Bagru — Block Print Town

A quick 25-minute drive from Jaipur, Bagru is famous across India for its traditional hand-block printed textiles. Ideal for shopping lovers — pick up authentic Rajasthani fabrics directly from artisan workshops.

Block Printing Textile Shopping

45 km · Your Best Stop on NH-48

🏨 Dudu — Hotel Highway Queen

The ideal midpoint. Breakfast from 5:30 AM, EV charging (CCS2), free parking for all vehicles, and 26 AC rooms for overnight stays. Directly on NH-48 — no detour required. This is the stop that makes the rest of the journey comfortable.

4.8★ Hotel EV Charging Restaurant Free Parking

75 km from Jaipur

💎 Kishangarh — Marble City

Known as the "Marble City of India," Kishangarh is where the highway reaches a crossroads. Visit the historic Kishangarh Fort overlooking Gundolav Lake — a stunning, off-the-beaten-track fortress with Aravalli views. The city's marble markets are worth a quick browse.

Kishangarh Fort Gundolav Lake Marble Market

105 km from Jaipur

🕌 Ajmer — Dargah Sharif

One of India's most sacred sites. The Ajmer Sharif Dargah of Sufi saint Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti draws pilgrims of every faith year-round. Also visit Ana Sagar Lake for a tranquil waterfront stroll, and the historic Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra mosque.

Ajmer Sharif Ana Sagar Lake Taragarh Fort

120 km from Jaipur · 15 km from Ajmer

🌊 Pushkar — Sacred Town

Just 15 km beyond Ajmer, Pushkar is home to the world's only Brahma Temple and the sacred Pushkar Lake surrounded by 52 bathing ghats. The town is especially magical during the annual Pushkar Camel Fair (November), drawing visitors from across the globe.

Brahma Temple Pushkar Lake Camel Fair (Nov)
