FAQ

Birthday Venue FAQ — Hotel Highway Queen

Common questions about our birthday celebration packages on NH-48, Dudu.

Is there a birthday party venue near the Jaipur-Ajmer highway?
Yes — Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu, NH-48 is the only dedicated birthday party venue on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway corridor. We are 45km from Jaipur and 60km from Ajmer, with decorated AC rooms, birthday cake, and full restaurant service available year-round.
What is included in the birthday party package?
All birthday packages include a fully decorated AC room (balloons, ribbon setup), a birthday cake, a welcome drink on arrival, 24-hour in-room dining access, and free parking for your vehicle. Add-ons like special cake flavours or extra decoration can be arranged via WhatsApp.
What is the price of a birthday package at Hotel Highway Queen?
Birthday packages start from ₹2,200 for a Deluxe AC room, ₹2,400 for a Balcony room, and ₹2,700 for a Super Deluxe room — all inclusive of decoration and cake. Call or WhatsApp +91-94144-49992 to confirm availability on your date.
How far is Hotel Highway Queen from Jaipur?
Hotel Highway Queen is located 45km from Jaipur on NH-48 (Jaipur–Mumbai National Highway) at Dudu, Rajasthan — approximately 45–50 minutes by road without traffic.
Can I book a birthday room for just one night on NH-48?
Yes, single-night birthday stays are our most popular request. Check in with full room decoration and cake, enjoy a birthday dinner at the restaurant, and check out the next morning. WhatsApp or call to check date availability.
Can Hotel Highway Queen host a group birthday celebration?
Absolutely. With 26 AC rooms and a large restaurant open from 5:30 AM, we comfortably host birthday groups of 10 to 50+ guests. Group booking discounts are available — contact us on WhatsApp to get a group rate.
Does Hotel Highway Queen have a restaurant for birthday dinners?
Yes. Our in-house restaurant is open from 5:30 AM and offers 24-hour in-room dining. We serve North Indian, Rajasthani, and highway-style meals — perfect for birthday lunches, dinners, or late-night celebrations.
Is parking available for birthday guests?
Yes — free, spacious highway-side parking for cars, SUVs, tempo travellers, and coaches. We also have a Mahindra CHARGE_iN EV fast charger (CCS2) on-site for electric vehicle guests.
How do I book a birthday package at Hotel Highway Queen?
The fastest way is to WhatsApp us or call +91-94144-49992. You can also book a room via Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, or Agoda — then mention "birthday package" to confirm decoration.
Is Hotel Highway Queen good for birthday travellers on the Delhi-Mumbai highway?
Yes. NH-48 is the Delhi to Mumbai National Highway. Hotel Highway Queen at Dudu is the ideal birthday stopover for road travellers on this route — with premium AC rooms, decoration, cake, full restaurant, and EV charging available around the clock.
