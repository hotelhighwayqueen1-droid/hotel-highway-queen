Dudu NH-48 hotel & restaurant: common questions

What is the address of Hotel Highway Queen?

Hotel Highway Queen is at NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008, on the Jaipur–Ajmer stretch of NH-48.

Is reception open 24 hours?

Yes. Reception is open 24 hours with hotel staff available. Call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 at any time.

Which is the best restaurant in Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant on NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur 303008 is rated 4.8★ from 1,000+ Google reviews. It serves Rajasthani thali, dedicated Jain food, South Indian, pizza, North Indian and chai, with free parking for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses.

Is Jain food available in Dudu?

Yes. The menu has a dedicated Jain section made without onion and garlic, which the hotel says is prepared separately.

Is late-night food available near Dudu on NH-48?

Hotel guests can order the full menu to their room 24/7. The Chinese section is served from 10 AM to 1 AM, and the restaurant itself is open 24 hours.

How much is a room at Hotel Highway Queen?

There are 26 AC rooms: Deluxe Room ₹2,200 + GST, Deluxe Room with Balcony ₹2,400 + GST and Super Deluxe Room ₹2,700 + GST per night, each with a 30% OFF offer. The hotel confirms offer dates and availability.

Is there parking for cars, trucks and buses?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses. Call +91 94144 49992 if you are bringing a large group or several buses.

Is EV charging available?

Yes. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is on site and open to any traveller on NH-48. Charging typically takes 20–45 minutes.
