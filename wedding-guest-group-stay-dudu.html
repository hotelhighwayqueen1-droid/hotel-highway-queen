Quick questions

How many rooms can I enquire about?

You can ask for up to 26 rooms, which is the total number of AC rooms. Availability depends on the dates, so the hotel cannot promise all 26 rooms on any date until staff confirm.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the enquiry?

No. The form only opens WhatsApp with your details, or you can call. Nothing is booked until hotel staff confirm rooms, dates and rates with you.

Are the rates inclusive of GST?

No. The original rates per night are Deluxe Room ₹2,200, Deluxe Room with Balcony ₹2,400 and Super Deluxe Room ₹2,700, all + GST.

What does the 30% OFF badge mean?

It shows that an offer is running on that room type. The hotel confirms offer dates, who can use it and room availability when you call or WhatsApp, so please check before you book.

Do you have a wedding hall, banquet, catering or decoration?

This page is for room accommodation. A wedding venue, banquet hall, catering package or event decoration is not listed here, and no special group discount is promised. Please ask reception what the hotel can confirm.

Can buses and large vehicles park?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses. Call +91-94144-49992 if you are bringing a large group or several buses.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Reception is open 24 hours with hotel staff available. Early check-in depends on availability.

Is the restaurant open 24 hours?

Yes, the restaurant is open 24 hours.
