Best Ajmer Sharif Ziyarat Tips:
Route, Timing & What to Carry

A local's guide to planning your Dargah ziyarat from Delhi or Jaipur via NH-48 — timing, dress, parking, and the perfect halt point.
🗺️

NH-48 Route Map

From Delhi, take NH-48 (Jaipur Expressway) through Gurugram → Shahjahanpur → Neemrana → Jaipur → Dudu (Hotel Highway Queen, halt point) → Kishangarh → Ajmer Sharif. The route is entirely smooth 4-lane highway. Avoid heavy traffic by leaving Delhi before 6 AM or after 9 PM.

⏰

Best Time to Visit

October to March offers the most comfortable weather for ziyarat. The Dargah opens at 4:00 AM daily and is most spiritually calm before 9 AM. The grand Urs of Khwaja Sahib is observed in the Islamic month of Rajab — the most spiritually significant but also the busiest period. Plan overnight stays for Urs visits.

👗

Dress Code & Etiquette

Women must cover their head with a dupatta or scarf and wear modest, full-length clothing. Men should wear full trousers and a kurta or clean shirt. Remove shoes at the Dargah gate. Do not point feet towards the mazaar. Recite Fatiha and Durood Sharif at the shrine. Photography inside the main dome is restricted.

🧳

What to Carry

Carry a clean dupatta (head cover), your tasbeeh, a copy of Surah Yasin or a small dua book, cash for chaddar and flowers at the Dargah. Comfortable walking shoes (removed at gates) are important. Bring a water bottle as it can get warm. A small bag for shoes is advisable. Do not carry leather products inside.

🚗

Parking at Ajmer Dargah

Parking near Ajmer Sharif Dargah is very limited, especially on Thursdays (Jumma) and during Urs. Use the designated parking lots near the Dargah outskirts and proceed by tanga (horse carriage) or on foot. Group pilgrims and bus travellers often park and rest at Hotel Highway Queen in Dudu (60 km away) before entering the city on smaller vehicles.

🕌

About the Dargah

Dargah Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (RA) is one of the holiest Muslim shrines in South Asia. Founded in the 13th century, it draws Sufi devotees of all faiths. The mazaar (tomb) is situated in the heart of Ajmer city. Important landmarks inside include Buland Darwaza, Nizam Gate, the two large degs (cauldrons), and the Shah Jahan mosque.
