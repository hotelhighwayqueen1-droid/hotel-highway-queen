Sambhar Lake Visit from Jaipur — Where to Stay on NH-48

Sambhar Lake is India's largest inland saltwater lake and a UNESCO Ramsar wetland site spanning over 200 square kilometers — and one of the most underrated day trips from Jaipur. Between November and February, tens of thousands of flamingos gather on the shallows, creating pink-tinged vistas that are unlike anything else in northern India. The lake also hosts bar-headed geese, cranes, and 150+ migratory species.

Hotel Highway Queen is 25 km from Sambhar Lake — approximately 30 minutes by road — making it the closest well-rated highway hotel to the lake on the NH-48 corridor. For a Sambhar Lake day trip from Jaipur, the ideal itinerary: check into Hotel Highway Queen the evening before, depart at 5:45 AM after a quick breakfast, reach Sambhar by 6:15 AM during golden hour, spend the morning, return to the hotel for lunch, and continue toward Jaipur or Ajmer in the afternoon.

This approach gives you 45-60 more minutes at the lake than a Jaipur city day trip — the difference between rushing and actually enjoying the experience. The hotel's free parking accommodates all vehicle types, and the early kitchen means you won't start your bird-watching day on an empty stomach.

Sambhar town itself has historical significance — a major Mughal-era trade center and home to the Shakambhari Mata temple on the lake's shore. Winter festival visitors can combine the temple with the flamingo spectacle in a single early morning outing. For EV drivers visiting Sambhar, the Mahindra CHARGE_iN station at Hotel Highway Queen provides a convenient top-up before or after the lake visit.
