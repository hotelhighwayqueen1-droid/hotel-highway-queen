What is dal baati churma and why is it famous on NH-48? +

Dal baati churma is Rajasthan's most iconic dish — and one of India's most celebrated regional foods. The meal has three components: baati, hard wheat balls baked in a wood-fired clay oven until golden and crunchy; dal, a five-lentil preparation slow-cooked with Rajasthani spices; and churma, coarsely ground wheat mixed with ghee and jaggery into a sweet crumble. Traditionally eaten with liberal amounts of desi ghee, it's the kind of meal that sustains a Rajasthani shepherd through an entire working day in the desert heat.

At Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48, dal baati churma is the centrepiece of the Rajasthani thali — prepared daily using traditional methods. Travellers frequently name it as the highlight of their entire NH-48 road trip.
