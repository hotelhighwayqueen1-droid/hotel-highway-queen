The Only Full-Menu 24-Hour Restaurant on Jaipur-Ajmer Highway
Hotel Highway Queen | Hotel & Restaurant at Dudu, NH-48 is the only 24-hour restaurant on the Jaipur-Ajmer highway that serves a complete hot menu — Rajasthani thali, North Indian, South Indian, Jain food, pizza, and chai — at any hour, any day, including at 2 AM.

If you have driven NH-48 at night, you know the problem: dhabas close by 11 PM, fast food is gone by midnight, and the next hour is a gamble. Whether you are a family arriving late from Delhi, a truck driver needing chai at 3 AM, or an early morning traveller catching the road before sunrise, Hotel Highway Queen | Hotel & Restaurant is the guaranteed stop where hot food is always ready.

Rated 4.8 stars with over 1,000 Google reviews, we are not just open — we are consistently good. The kitchen is staffed and cooking through every shift so you never wait long and never get reheated food. From a full Rajasthani thali to a wood-fired pizza or a comforting bowl of dal-chawal, the menu does not shrink at midnight. That is the Hotel Highway Queen difference on the Jaipur-Ajmer highway.

Located precisely on NH-48 at Ram Nagar, Dudu — midway between Jaipur (45 km) and Ajmer (60 km) — this is the natural halfway point where hunger and highway fatigue meet their match. Pull over, park free (trucks and buses welcome), and eat properly before continuing your journey.
