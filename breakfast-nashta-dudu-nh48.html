Where can I have breakfast early morning on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway near Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48 at Dudu serves breakfast from 5:30 AM, on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. The restaurant is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, and the whole kitchen is pure vegetarian. The menu includes tawa aloo paratha with dahi, chole bhature, idli sambhar, masala tea and lassi, with free parking for cars, trucks and buses.

Breakfast FAQ

Which restaurant opens earliest on NH-48 near Dudu?

Hotel Highway Queen Restaurant on NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu serves breakfast from 5:30 AM, and the restaurant itself is open 24 hours, 7 days a week. That makes it a practical stop for travellers who leave before sunrise on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway. Please call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 to confirm before you travel.

What time does breakfast start?

Breakfast starts from 5:30 AM, and the restaurant is open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Hotel guests can also order the full menu to their room around the clock through in-room dining. Timings can change, so please call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 if you are travelling very early and want to confirm before you set out.

What breakfast dishes are available?

The menu includes tawa aloo paratha with dahi, other stuffed and tawa parathas, chole bhature, puri bhaji, idli sambhar, pav bhaji, bread butter and sandwiches. For drinks there are masala tea, coffee, chhach (buttermilk), Queen Special Lassi and cold coffee. See the morning menu board on this page, or call +91 94144 49992 to ask what is ready.

What does breakfast cost?

Tawa Aloo Paratha with Dahi is ₹180, Chole Bhature is ₹190, Idli Sambhar is ₹160, Masala Tea is ₹35 and Queen Special Lassi is ₹90. Prices are exclusive of GST and subject to change, so please confirm current rates with the staff. The full price list is on the morning menu board above and on the full menu page.

Is it pure veg?

Yes. Hotel Highway Queen is a pure vegetarian restaurant, and every dish on the menu is vegetarian, including all breakfast items. The kitchen also has a separate Jain section made without onion and garlic. See our pure veg restaurant page for more, or call +91 94144 49992 if you have a question before you arrive.

Is the restaurant open 24 hours?

Yes. The restaurant is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, with breakfast from 5:30 AM. The Chinese section is served from 10 AM to 1 AM. For late-night and 24-hour dining details on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway, see our 24-hour restaurant page, or call or WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 to confirm before you travel.

Is parking free for cars, trucks and buses?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is also on site and open to any traveller on NH-48. Call +91 94144 49992 if you are bringing a large group or several buses. See our truck and bus driver page for more.

Can I pre-order on WhatsApp?

Yes. You can pre-order on WhatsApp at +91 94144 49992 by sending your name, the number of people and what you would like to eat. A WhatsApp message is an enquiry and not a confirmed order, so wait for the restaurant to reply. Please call the same number if you are arriving very early.
