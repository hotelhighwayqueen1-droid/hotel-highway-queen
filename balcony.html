Quick answer: The Deluxe Balcony Room at Hotel Highway Queen costs ₹2,400 per night plus GST. Our Deluxe with Balcony room is for those who love a breath of fresh air with their morning chai. This beautifully appointed room comes with a private balcony overlooking the scenic Jaipur–Ajmer Highway, giving you a sense of openness and freedom rare in any hotel stay. With stylish décor, premium comfort and all modern amenities, it balances the great outdoors with indoor luxury.

Hotel Highway Queen sits directly on the highway, so there is no detour: park free, check in, and order hot chai and breakfast to the room — the restaurant opens at 5:30 AM and in-room dining runs 24/7.

FAQ

Balcony Room — common questions

What is the price of the Balcony Room?

The Deluxe Balcony Room starts at ₹2,400 per night plus applicable GST. Book direct on WhatsApp at +91-94144-49992 or via Agoda, Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Expedia or Goibibo.

Where is Hotel Highway Queen located?

NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008 — about 45 km from Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer on the Jaipur–Ajmer highway.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Reception is open 24/7, so late-night and early-morning arrivals are welcome; early check-in depends on availability.

Does the Balcony Room have a private balcony and AC?

Yes. The Deluxe Balcony Room has a private balcony overlooking the Jaipur–Ajmer highway, air conditioning with individual temperature control, free high-speed WiFi, a Smart LED TV, an attached bathroom and a king or queen-size bed.

Is laundry service available in the Balcony Room?

Yes. Laundry is available on request for guests in the Balcony Room.

Is parking free, and can trucks and buses park?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for cars, SUVs, trucks and buses.

Is there EV charging at the hotel?

Yes. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 fast charger is on-site and open to any traveller on NH-48; room booking is optional.

Can I order food to the room at night?

Yes. The full restaurant menu, including Rajasthani thali and Jain food, is available as 24/7 in-room dining. The restaurant opens at 5:30 AM.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before check-in. Valid government photo ID is required at check-in. Pets are not allowed and smoking is not permitted inside rooms. More answers: full hotel FAQ.
