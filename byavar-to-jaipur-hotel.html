NH-48 Route Breakdown: Byavar to Jaipur

Via Kishangarh → Dudu | Total approx. 135–145 km | 2.5–3 hours by car

Byavar
Your starting point on NH-48 in Ajmer district. Byavar is a key commercial town on the Jaipur–Ajmer corridor. From here, you're approximately 135 km from Jaipur — making Dudu the smart midpoint stop before the final Jaipur stretch.
Beawar (~10 km from Byavar)
A small fuel and snack stop on NH-48 just after Byavar. Continue past toward Kishangarh on the expressway.
Kishangarh (~40 km from Byavar)
Rajasthan's marble city and the gateway to the Jaipur–Kishangarh 6-lane expressway. Notable for Kishangarh Fort, the Miniature Painting School, and Asia's largest marble market. Fuel up here if needed.
📍 Dudu — Hotel Highway Queen (~75–80 km from Byavar)
Your stop. Hotel Highway Queen sits directly on NH-48 at Dudu, Ram Nagar — 45 km before Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer. Whether you need an overnight stay, a hot meal starting at 5:30 AM, EV charging, or safe parking for trucks and buses, this is the NH-48 highway hotel to stop at.

Jaipur (~45 km from Dudu)
Rajasthan's Pink City — Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, Jantar Mantar, City Palace. After your break at Hotel Highway Queen, you're a smooth 35–45 minute drive from Jaipur city.
