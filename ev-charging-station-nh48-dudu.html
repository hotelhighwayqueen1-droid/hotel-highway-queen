FAQs

EV Charging on NH48 — Common Questions

Is there EV charging on NH48 between Jaipur and Ajmer?
Yes. Hotel Highway Queen on NH-48 at Dudu operates a Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 EV charging station, roughly 45 km from Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer.
What type of EV charger is available here?
A Mahindra CHARGE_iN DC fast charger with a CCS2 connector — the standard fast-charging port used by most electric cars sold in India.
Which EVs are compatible with the CCS2 charger?
Mahindra BE6, Mahindra XEV9e, Tata Nexon EV, Tata Punch EV, MG ZS EV, and most other CCS2-equipped electric cars.
How long does it take to charge an EV here?
Around 20 to 45 minutes for a typical fast charge, depending on your vehicle's battery size and charging capability.
Is the charging station open 24 hours?
Yes, the CCS2 point is accessible round the clock, and in-room dining also runs 24 hours for late-night travellers.
Do I need to book a room to use the EV charger?
No — the charging station is open to any traveller on NH-48, room booking is optional.
What can I do while my EV charges?
Dine at the restaurant, rest in the lounge, freshen up, or book a room — the Charge + Dine + Rest combo is built around the 20–45 minute window.
Is parking free for EV charging customers?
Yes, parking is free for all vehicles, including cars using the EV charger, and trucks and buses are welcome too.
How far is this stop from Jaipur and Ajmer?
About 45 km from Jaipur and 60 km from Ajmer on NH-48 — close to the midpoint of that stretch.
Can I charge and get food or a room at the same stop?
Yes — restaurant from 5:30 AM, 24-hour in-room dining, and 26 AC rooms from ₹2,200, all on the same NH-48 property.
