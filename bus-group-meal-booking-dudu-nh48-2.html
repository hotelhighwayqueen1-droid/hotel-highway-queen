How group booking works

Four steps, about a minute for the group leader.

  1. Send group size and arrival time

    WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 with how many people, your date and expected arrival time, and how many are veg or Jain.

  2. Choose the menu

    Pick a thali, a Jain meal, a snack and chai stop or a breakfast option from the menu below.

  3. Get confirmation

    The hotel replies on WhatsApp with the menu, per-head rate and timing for your group.

  4. Arrive, sit and eat

    Park free, walk in, and be seated and served quickly because the kitchen already knows you are coming.

EV charging on NH-48

A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is on site and open to any traveller on NH-48. Charging typically takes 20–45 minutes. Pay with the CHARGE_iN app, Me4U or other aggregator apps. The charger is available 24/7 and needs no advance booking.

Any EV with a CCS2 connector can charge, for example the Mahindra BE 6, XEV 9e and XUV400 EV, Tata Nexon EV and Punch EV, and MG ZS EV. Eat, rest or stay while you charge.

Group booking and stay: common questions

How do I book a group meal in Dudu on NH-48?

WhatsApp +91 94144 49992 with your group size, arrival date and time, and the veg and Jain count. The hotel confirms the menu, rate and timing. You can also call the same number.

Is there parking for tour buses at Hotel Highway Queen?

Yes. Parking is free and secure for buses, trucks, cars and SUVs. If you are bringing several buses, call +91 94144 49992 before you leave so space is ready.

Is the restaurant open at night for late or early buses?

Yes. The restaurant is open 24 hours, so night and early-morning arrivals can be served. Send your arrival time on WhatsApp so the kitchen is ready.

Is pure veg and Jain food available for groups?

Yes. The whole menu is 100% vegetarian. The Jain section is made without onion and garlic and, as the hotel states, prepared separately. Tell us how many in your group need Jain food.

What is the price per head for a group meal?

The Queen Spl. Thali is listed at ₹365 + GST. Other menu prices exclude GST too. For a group, the hotel confirms the final per-head rate on WhatsApp. We accept cash, UPI, and debit or credit cards.

How much advance notice is needed and how many people can you seat?

Send your details as early as you can. The hotel confirms seating and timing for your group size on WhatsApp, so please share the number of people when you enquire.

Do drivers and conductors get a meal?

Drivers and conductors are welcome, and the menu has thali, parathas and chai. Ask about the driver and conductor meal arrangement when you book on WhatsApp.

Where exactly is the restaurant on the Jaipur–Ajmer route?

Hotel Highway Queen is at NH-48, Ram Nagar, Dudu, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303008. It is about 65 km from Jaipur city centre, 60 km from Ajmer Sharif Dargah and 30 km from Kishangarh.

Can my group stay overnight at Hotel Highway Queen?

Yes, subject to availability. The hotel has 26 AC rooms. Send your dates and the number of guests on WhatsApp or call, and the hotel confirms rooms and rates. Reception is open 24 hours.

How much are the rooms, and what does the 30% OFF mean?

Deluxe Room ₹2,200, Deluxe Room with Balcony ₹2,400 and Super Deluxe Room ₹2,700 per night, all + GST. The 30% OFF badge shows that an offer is running on that room type. The rates shown are the original rates; the hotel confirms offer dates, who can use it and availability when you call or WhatsApp.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Reception is open 24 hours. Early check-in depends on availability. A valid government photo ID is required at check-in.

Is EV charging available at the hotel?

Yes. A Mahindra CHARGE_iN CCS2 DC fast charger is on site and open to any traveller on NH-48. Charging typically takes 20–45 minutes. Pay with the CHARGE_iN app, Me4U or aggregator apps.

Is WiFi free?

Yes. Free high-speed WiFi is available in the rooms, lobby and restaurant.

What is the cancellation policy for room bookings?

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 24 hours may not be eligible for a refund, and no-shows and early check-outs are non-refundable.

How can we pay, and are menu prices inclusive of GST?
